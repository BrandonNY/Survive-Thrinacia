Current rules

2 actions per day.

Fishing: starts at 5–20. Each consecutive fishing action raises the minimum roll by 2. Skip a full day of fishing and the difficulty drops by 2.

Cows: first attempt needs 11–20. After one failure, the next cow attempt needs 14–20. Fail that second attempt and you are caught; 2 crew members die.

Ship: wood first goes into inventory. Repair Ship installs up to 4 wood per action. Each repair action costs 5% hunger. Install 30 wood for 100% repair and keep at least 5 crew alive.

Food: 1 day of food is consumed at the end of each day. With no food, hunger drops 20% per day. Below 20% hunger, 1 crew member dies each day.
