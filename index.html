📖 How to Survive

2 actions per day. The day advances only after you confirm it.

Wild food: collect safe food for 15 seconds; avoid poisonous food. Earn up to 1.5 food days.

Birds: 5 stones to hit moving birds; earn up to 1.5 food days.

Fishing: keep the moving fish inside your green bar. Every consecutive attempt shrinks the bar; skip a full day and it becomes easier.

Cows: guide your sailor through a changing maze to the cow while avoiding Helios. Every time Helios catches you, 2 crew members die. After a failed attempt, he moves faster.

Ship: wood first goes into inventory. Repair Ship opens a moving timing bar: land up to 3 good hits to install up to 4 wood. Each attempt costs 5% hunger. Install 30 wood for 100% repair and keep at least 5 crew alive.

Food: 1 day of food is consumed at the end of each day. With no food, hunger drops 20% per day. Below 20% hunger, 1 crew member dies each day.
